OmniTerm / Offline review prototype / Entirely fictional

What did the work deliver?

Three synthetic outcomes. Each provider finished its turn and each notice was delivered and read. A person's decision about the work remains separate.

Every task, number, evidence note, PR reference and decision below is invented. Nothing is connected to an agent, repository or account.
